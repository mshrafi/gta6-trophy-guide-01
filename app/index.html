One trophy can be missed

Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at every spot) is the only trophy the guides flag as possibly missable: one meditation spot is only reachable in Chapter 1. Meditate at every spot you find in Chapter 1 before the story moves on — the rest can be visited later. Jump to it ↓

Flagged by GTABase and PowerPyx, via EGamers and GTAScoop. Story trophies cannot be missed — they unlock as the story plays.

Roadmap to the Platinum

Six stages, in order. Guides put the full run at around 200 hours; the story alone takes 50–60. Do Stage 1's meditation spots as you go — everything else can wait until after the credits.
